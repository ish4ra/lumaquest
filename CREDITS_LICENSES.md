## Current 3D milestone assets

The current Green Fields 3D milestone intentionally builds its visible character, enemy and environment geometry procedurally from Godot primitive meshes and original colors/material definitions in this repository.

The legacy SVG concept/prototype assets under `assets/pixel/` are original project assets retained for historical/concept reference.
